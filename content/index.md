# The Hollow City

 ### **[Roll 20](https://app.roll20.net/campaigns/details/20526469/the-hollow-city)**    **[Game Guide](https://docs.google.com/document/d/19Ob6iqQ9L1OeO8qVTQ8RIMZkOlQ6yi405lgaDQt7kuI/edit?usp=sharing)**     [Discord](https://discordapp.com/channels/1037400306058801263/1401764492320182455) 
---
Once, the city of [[Locations/Kamor]] thrived as a hub of immortal rulers and arcane knowledge. Now it is silent, its streets empty, its towers overgrown. Whispers speak of a curse, of immortals turned to shadows, and of secrets that could undo the fabric of life itself. The party must explore the abandoned city, uncover why its rulers vanished, and decide whether to awaken its denizens or let the city’s legacy die.

We learn that they are meant to be meeting some more travelers at a crossroads under the invitation of a man named Sindri. The men discuss their reasons for wanting to go to Kamor, Damian mentions that the Iron Roses are there, Xan notes that Umaemar is also there. A few days later, we meet Marlowe and Sylvia at the crossroads and Xan and Damian's carriage approaches. We learn that Marlowe used to live in Kamor. A fair distance away, and another few days later, we meet Rathil, Cazar and Nerola as they approach a camp from Lyde. They meet Sindri, Orna, and Floyd. Orna is a historian and Floyd an arcanist. As the others arrive, we learn we must retrieve four parts of an arcane key, one is already in Sindri's possession, one was stolen by goblins, one is in the possession of a merchant which Hershal is tracking, and the last is in some kind of hoard guarded by kobalds. The next day, the party sets out to the goblin's camp. They defeat the goblins and find the key. |
