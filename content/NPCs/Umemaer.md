## Description

An elvish Monk. He had chocolaty dark skin, was tall and slender and yet well built and his eyes were a piercing green. Though he was obviously old, he was not frail, strength emanated from him. He wore a black gi which was intricately adorned with golden filigree that danced upon the folds and flaps of the fabric he lowered his hood revealing his pointed elvish ears and bald head.

[[NPCs/Umemaer]] trained [[PCs/Xanotteron Ti'vahl O'lócë\|Xan]] but was also effectively a father to him as he grew up.
