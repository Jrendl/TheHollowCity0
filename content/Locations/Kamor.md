Once hailed as the [[Locations/Kamor\|City of Stars]], [[Locations/Kamor]] was a glittering metropolis of brass towers, stained glass, and spell-lit streets where invention and sorcery intertwined so seamlessly that the boundary between them dissolved. Suspended over the chasm of the [[Locations/Aurelian Rift]] by vast rune-forged silver chains, [[Locations/Kamor]] was a marvel of both arcane engineering and ambition; a city that hummed with energy day and night, powered by the mysterious “[[Lore/Aether Core]]” buried deep beneath its central spire.

- Two years ago, the humming stopped.

- Every clock froze at the same instant. Every lantern guttered out. When traders from the surrounding nations finally dared to cross the bridges, they found [[Locations/Kamor]] silent; its markets still stocked, its tea still warm, its citizens gone without a trace. No bodies. No signs of struggle. Only echoes of a once great city.

- Today, [[Locations/Kamor]] stands as a haunting monument to vanished brilliance. Automatons still wander the streets, still on guard for any bold enough to seek to collect the Hollow Cities riches.

- The [[Lore/Aether Core]] below still pulses faintly, though none who have descended to study it have returned. Scholars debate whether [[Locations/Kamor]]’s people ascended, imploded, or were drawn into the very spellwork that once empowered their city.
