## Humble Origins
[[PCs/Xanotteron Ti'vahl O'lócë\|Xan]]  was born to parents [[NPCs/Munob O'lócë\|Munob]] and [[NPCs/Kadau O'lócë\|Kadau]] in the city of [[Locations/Kamor]]. [[NPCs/Munob O'lócë\|Munob]] and [[NPCs/Kadau O'lócë\|Kadau]] admired their child, he had the same dark brown eyes as his father and beautiful facial structure of his mother. His skin was pale just like his parents. For almost a year, the parents lovingly raised their little one. Their kindness seeped into him even though he was not yet able to speak. Not long after his birth, [[PCs/Xanotteron Ti'vahl O'lócë\|Xan]] began walking, and then quickly running. He was naturally athletic and could barely be contained.

One fateful day in the following year, only seven months after [[PCs/Xanotteron Ti'vahl O'lócë\|Xan]]'s birth, the family found themselves approached by an elvish man. He had chocolaty dark skin, was tall and slender and yet well built and his eyes were a piercing green. Though he was obviously old, he was not frail, strength emanated from him. As he approached the family's abode, he wore a black gi which was intricately adorned with golden filigree that danced upon the folds and flaps of the fabric. He lowered his hood revealing his pointed elvish ears and bald head. [[NPCs/Munob O'lócë\|Munob]] and [[NPCs/Kadau O'lócë\|Kadau]] had not seen this man before, though they know that it could only be one person.

This was [[NPCs/Umemaer]]; a reclusive monk who lived in a monastery deep in the [[Locations/Arcwood]] and trained monks to be protectors of the land and the people. [[NPCs/Umemaer]] was said to be an ancient elf who had seen and done much, but he was not often seen by those on the outside. He brought with him the knowledge of a generational martial art, one which he taught to children to whom he had been given visions of. These, however, were just rumors, as not many, other than the families of the those chosen, had seen [[NPCs/Umemaer]]. But upon the sight of this man approaching their home, [[NPCs/Munob O'lócë\|Munob]] and [[NPCs/Kadau O'lócë\|Kadau]] knew exactly what was to come.

Even though they were anxious at the thought of what this meeting was to bring, the parents invited [[NPCs/Umemaer]] into their home and offered him a cup of tea, which he graciously accepted. [[NPCs/Umemaer]] explained to [[NPCs/Munob O'lócë\|Munob]] and [[NPCs/Kadau O'lócë\|Kadau]] how he was a representative of [[Lore/The Monks of the Tangled Root]], a group of monks dedicated to the protection of the cycle of life and knowledge under the guidance of the [[Lore/Arctree]]. [[NPCs/Umemaer]] claimed he was given a vision of [[PCs/Xanotteron Ti'vahl O'lócë\|Xan]] by the [[Lore/Arctree]], that [[PCs/Xanotteron Ti'vahl O'lócë\|Xanotteron]] would become a great warrior under the guidance of the [[Lore/The Monks of the Tangled Root]].  [[NPCs/Umemaer]] would take [[PCs/Xanotteron Ti'vahl O'lócë\|Xan]] under his wing and raise him to be considerate of all beings, but yet a fierce warrior. He assured the couple that they would not lose their child, that he would return to them and the world in some time. Though they were hesitant to let their child go, and rightfully so, they trusted [[NPCs/Umemaer]], and they knew that if [[PCs/Xanotteron Ti'vahl O'lócë\|Xan]] was destined for this path they were not to stand in its way. [[NPCs/Umemaer]] left with the baby, and brought him home, a monastery deep in the depths of the [[Locations/Arcwood]], the ancient home of the [[Lore/Arctree]].

## A Purposeful Childhood
[[PCs/Xanotteron Ti'vahl O'lócë\|Xan]] was raised well by [[NPCs/Umemaer]]. By day, he was trained in nature and survival, maths and science, arcana, and, of course, martial combat. [[NPCs/Umemaer]] was always kind and gentle yet firm with [[PCs/Xanotteron Ti'vahl O'lócë\|Xan]]. He taught him strength in kindness to the plants and animals around him. By night, [[NPCs/Umemaer]] trained [[PCs/Xanotteron Ti'vahl O'lócë\|Xan]] in the ways of weapon smithing. He taught that, though they did not use bladed weapons as commonly as their fists, it was their job to understand their intricacies, strengths, and weaknesses. This backwards-forwards approach of learning your enemy was used in much of [[PCs/Xanotteron Ti'vahl O'lócë\|Xan]]'s training. It instilled within him a respect for the ways of others and the knowledge and power to dismantle his enemy. [[PCs/Xanotteron Ti'vahl O'lócë\|Xan]] grew close to many other children in [[Locations/The Tangled Root]], his brothers and sisters, but he also grew to love [[NPCs/Umemaer]] as a father.

Once [[PCs/Xanotteron Ti'vahl O'lócë\|Xan]] mastered hand-to-hand combat, [[NPCs/Umemaer]] moved his training on to combat with beasts. [[PCs/Xanotteron Ti'vahl O'lócë\|Xan]] learned how to weave and dodge around a large monster, how to out-smart a dexterous and small one, and even how to stupefy an intelligent beast. He learned techniques not only for besting beasts, but for controlling them. He was charged with raising several small animals to learn their habits and instincts, another example of the reverse teaching style of [[NPCs/Umemaer]].

In parallel with his combat training and his schooling, [[NPCs/Umemaer]] taught him the art of meditation, a core practice in the religion of the [[Lore/Arctree]]. This is a skill that [[PCs/Xanotteron Ti'vahl O'lócë\|Xan]] quickly picked up on. The ability to shut off one's mind to the physical world and detach oneself from the confines of their body. It was through meditation that [[PCs/Xanotteron Ti'vahl O'lócë\|Xan]] had visions of bright colors, abstract places, winding time, and monsters he had never seen the likes of. 

While training, [[PCs/Xanotteron Ti'vahl O'lócë\|Xan]] also began to get close with many of his peers at [[Locations/The Tangled Root]]. One such peer, was a cleric of the [[Lore/Arctree]] named [[NPCs/Agis Qinqwarin\|Agis]]. [[NPCs/Agis Qinqwarin\|Agis]] was a few years older than [[PCs/Xanotteron Ti'vahl O'lócë\|Xan]], but they got along well. [[NPCs/Agis Qinqwarin\|Agis]] and [[PCs/Xanotteron Ti'vahl O'lócë\|Xan]] spent many weekend days and nights with each other, exploring the nearby forest and playing games with the other younglings. Over the years, the two got very close, which made it all the more difficult when [[NPCs/Agis Qinqwarin\|Agis]] was called away on a their first long-duration adventure. Assigned to accompany an adventuring party called [[Lore/The Iron Roses]] on their quest, [[PCs/Xanotteron Ti'vahl O'lócë\|Xan]] was both jealous and lonely while they were away for over a year. He had yet to have the chance to venture beyond the forest, though had been on plenty a quest to quell an uprising of monsters from within the forest. 

Eventually, [[NPCs/Agis Qinqwarin\|Agis]] returned with an unfamiliar figure. A young Human-Nephilim named [[PCs/Damian]] who had been recovered from a nearby cult. [[NPCs/Agis Qinqwarin\|Agis]] stayed for a short time, but had to return to his quest with [[Lore/The Iron Roses]]. For three years, [[PCs/Damian]] stayed at [[Locations/The Tangled Root]] and [[PCs/Xanotteron Ti'vahl O'lócë\|Xan]] and him became good friends. [[PCs/Xanotteron Ti'vahl O'lócë\|Xan]] was a few years older than [[PCs/Damian]] so he immediately took him under his wing. Teaching him the ways of the monks, though not forcing them upon him once he protested. [[PCs/Xanotteron Ti'vahl O'lócë\|Xan]] felt responsible for [[PCs/Damian]] for quite some time. He eventually forged a fine blade for him, as he saw that [[PCs/Damian]] was taking an interest in swordplay. It was too large for [[PCs/Damian]] at first, but he grew into it. [[PCs/Damian]] studied alongside [[PCs/Xanotteron Ti'vahl O'lócë\|Xan]] under [[NPCs/Umemaer]]. He was a fast learner, specifically in the ways of the arcane, leading [[NPCs/Umemaer]] to suggest that he continue his studies with one of the Clerics. 

The two boys bond made it all the more difficult when [[Lore/Zor'ath]] finally caught up with [[PCs/Damian]]. Late one night, [[PCs/Xanotteron Ti'vahl O'lócë\|Xan]] was working in the forge when he noticed some of the pilgrims who had taken refuge at [[Locations/The Tangled Root]] sneaking around. He watched them enter [[PCs/Damian]]'s room and capture him. [[PCs/Xanotteron Ti'vahl O'lócë\|Xan]] quickly fetched [[NPCs/Umemaer]] and a few other of the masters and they were able to apprehend the cultists, but [[PCs/Damian]] was afraid. He did not want to put those at [[Locations/The Tangled Root]] in danger and so, against the wishes of [[PCs/Xanotteron Ti'vahl O'lócë\|Xan]] and the others, he left. That was the last time [[PCs/Xanotteron Ti'vahl O'lócë\|Xan]] would see him for a while.

## Crisis

It was years later when everything changed. [[PCs/Xanotteron Ti'vahl O'lócë\|Xan]] was in his mid 20's when news of the fall of [[Locations/Kamor]] reached [[Locations/The Tangled Root]]. While this news was upsetting, it didn't reach the depths that it would have with [[PCs/Xanotteron Ti'vahl O'lócë\|Xan]] had he known that that's where he was from. However, [[NPCs/Umemaer]] did know. Not yet ready to tell [[PCs/Xanotteron Ti'vahl O'lócë\|Xan]] that his parents might be dead, [[NPCs/Umemaer]] volunteered himself for the quest being sent to [[Locations/Kamor]] to investigate, hoping to get some concrete evidence on the state of [[NPCs/Munob O'lócë\|Munob]] and [[NPCs/Kadau O'lócë\|Kadau]]. Again, [[PCs/Xanotteron Ti'vahl O'lócë\|Xan]] protested, he did not want to see another important figure in his life leave, asking if he could at least go with, but [[NPCs/Umemaer]] went anyways, assuring [[PCs/Xanotteron Ti'vahl O'lócë\|Xan]] he'd be ok.

He was not.

It was just 2 months later when the rest of the party returned from [[Locations/Kamor]] with little news about the incident itself, but a tragic loss of [[NPCs/Umemaer]]. [[PCs/Xanotteron Ti'vahl O'lócë\|Xan]] took the loss harshly. He went silent for weeks, meditating in his forge, eating barely anything. He was frail and week by the time he finally arose. He went straight to the council of elders and informed them that he would be leaving to search for [[NPCs/Umemaer]]'s body. The council advised against it, but [[PCs/Xanotteron Ti'vahl O'lócë\|Xan]] went anyways. 

## On the road

He set off alone in the night, carrying nothing but his GI, some commoner's clothes, a sickle and some shuriken he forged, some rations, and his quarterstaff, which he had carved alongside [[NPCs/Umemaer]] in one of their earliest lessons. He set out on foot, wandering for a few months with no real intention of heading towards [[Locations/Kamor]] for at least a while. He stopped in small towns, got belligerently drunk at taverns, got in a fist fight or two, and just kept walking. He let his hair grow long and unkempt and almost never bathed. [[NPCs/Umemaer]] was his only family, he had been left again and again throughout his life and he had finally broken. He cared too much. He wasn't strong enough to protect him. He wasn't wise enough to accept his loss. Death was just a soul returning to the roots was it not? He should be happy for [[NPCs/Umemaer]]! He should celebrate his cycle! But all he had the strength to do was drink himself into a violent stupor and punch the shit out of some obese orcs. 

One night, almost a year into his drunken rampage, one of the orcs he beat up the night before found him in a back alley, asleep in a pile of his own vomit. The orc woke [[PCs/Xanotteron Ti'vahl O'lócë\|Xan]] up and introduced himself as [[NPCs/Kargh]]. [[NPCs/Kargh]] took [[PCs/Xanotteron Ti'vahl O'lócë\|Xan]] in, nursing him back to health and helping him reach sobriety after a few months. Once [[PCs/Xanotteron Ti'vahl O'lócë\|Xan]] had fully returned to his senses, he knew he had to find his way to [[Locations/Kamor]] and complete what he had set out to do. Luckily, the town where [[NPCs/Kargh]] lived was only a few days trek. [[NPCs/Kargh]] told [[PCs/Xanotteron Ti'vahl O'lócë\|Xan]] to find a man name [[NPCs/Sindri]] outside of [[Locations/Kamor]] and that he'd have a way to get in. 

[[PCs/Xanotteron Ti'vahl O'lócë\|Xan]] made the journey, a new sense of urgency bubbling up inside of him. It was time he knew the truth, and something was telling him this would be a very important discovery indeed...
