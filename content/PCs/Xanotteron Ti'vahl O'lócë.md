## Description
[[PCs/Xanotteron Ti'vahl O'lócë\|Xan]] is a slender-built and live elf in his id 20's. He's about 5'10" and relatively thin. His shoulder-length, dark hair pulls back in a half bun, slightly exposing his tall, pointy ears. His face is well built and stately and he has warm brown eyes that look inviting. His arms and shoulders are strong from long days in the forge and his back and torso are well defined from grueling exercises and practice. His body is covered in faint scars and burn marks, both from the forge and from the training he has undergone as a monk.

When the party meets him, [[PCs/Xanotteron Ti'vahl O'lócë\|Xan]] wheres travelers clothes which are worn in through months on the road. He carries a long quarter-staff carved of dark oak wood with intricate twisting patterns carved into it like vines wrapping around the staff. The grooves and pathways give way to knots and shapes near the top, reflecting animals and beasts. Throughout the artwork on the staff is gold imprints which highlight the sculptural elements. Along with the staff, he carries a quite normal looking, though well crafted, sickle, and a pack with some supplies. Beyond that he is quite minimally adorned. 

Unannounced to the party, in his pack he carries with him an intricate Gi of black cloth adorned with golden filigree mimicking the branching and twisting patterns of a tree.
