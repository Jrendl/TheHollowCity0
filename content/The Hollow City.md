# The Hollow City

 ### **[Roll 20](https://app.roll20.net/campaigns/details/20526469/the-hollow-city)**    **[Game Guide](https://docs.google.com/document/d/19Ob6iqQ9L1OeO8qVTQ8RIMZkOlQ6yi405lgaDQt7kuI/edit?usp=sharing)**     [Discord](https://discordapp.com/channels/1037400306058801263/1401764492320182455) 
---
Once, the city of [[Locations/Kamor]] thrived as a hub of immortal rulers and arcane knowledge. Now it is silent, its streets empty, its towers overgrown. Whispers speak of a curse, of immortals turned to shadows, and of secrets that could undo the fabric of life itself. The party must explore the abandoned city, uncover why its rulers vanished, and decide whether to awaken its denizens or let the city’s legacy die.
