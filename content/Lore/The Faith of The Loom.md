## Description
[[Lore/The Faith of The Loom]] teaches that all existence is woven from six divine threads spun upon the Loom of Eternity. Every being, object, and moment is a pattern in this Grand Tapestry, bound by the interplay of creation, decay, order, chaos, solace, and connection. Worshipers choose which threads to align their lives with, some weaving themselves to a single thread, others embracing the entire pattern.

## The Six Threads:
Six Threads formed from the Divine Loom, the Six Divine Threads are as follows.

| Thread | Aspect     | Color  |
| ------ | ---------- | ------ |
| Vita   | Creation   | White  |
| Emphis | Decay      | Black  |
| Solen  | Connection | Yellow |
| Citra  | Solace     | Purple |
| Orn    | Order      | Red    |
| Vaas   | Chaos      | Green  |  
  

## Beliefs:

### Living Tapestry
- The universe is a living tapestry; every life and action is a stitch.

### The Great Game

- Each god tugs at their thread, drawing the Divine Loom toward their own purpose. The six divine wills strain against one another, and the Loom itself quivers beneath the pressure. Most followers pray that the weave endures; yet a few zealots and prophets whisper that if the tapestry were ever to snap, creation might be remade; cleansed of imperfection and born anew. To worship is to pull. Every act of devotion, every prayer, every offering places weight upon a chosen thread, tightening the connection between mortal and divine. The faithful do not merely praise their gods; they join in the weaving, for good or ill.
    

  

## Tenets:

### I. Living Tapestry: 

- All acts, good or ill, shape the tapestry. Choose your thread and weave with intention.

### II. Play the Great Game

- Our actions help determine the threads made by the Divine Loom, ensure your actions pull with purpose, for every choice forms the Grand Tapestry. 
  

## Religious Practices

### Talisman

- Followers often wear woven talismans of colored threads symbolizing their chosen gods.
