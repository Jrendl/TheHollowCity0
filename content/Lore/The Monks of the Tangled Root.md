 [[Lore/The Monks of the Tangled Root]] are an apolitical group of monks dedicated to protecting the cycle and sanctity of life and knowledge. They live at [[Locations/The Tangled Root]], a monastery on the northeast edge of the [[Locations/Arcwood]] They closely follow the [[Lore/Arctree]]. They often work at the behest of local governments and peoples in attempt to better the lands and lives of those around them. They stay out of political affairs and wars in attempt to remain a neutral and safe party for all peoples.
