The [[Lore/Arctree]] is the sacred deity of [[Lore/Arcism]]. 

The [[Lore/Arctree]] is a sentient, divine being whose roots and branches extend into all realms. Every living thing is a part of its vast network, connected by unseen threads of essence. To harm nature is to wound the [[Lore/Arctree]] itself; to nurture life is to strengthen the divine.
