## Description:

Arcism teaches that the [[Lore/Arctree]] is a sentient, divine being whose roots and branches extend into all realms. Every living thing is a part of its vast network, connected by unseen threads of essence. To harm nature is to wound the [[Lore/Arctree]] itself; to nurture life is to strengthen the divine.

Followers believe that the [[Lore/Arctree]] roots brought the world into being; that all growth, decay, and rebirth are reflections of its endless cycles of awakening and slumber. [[Lore/Arcists]] see no divide between the natural and the sacred; life itself is worship.

In Arcism, the Seasons are not merely the passage of time; they are the breath of the [[Lore/Arctree]], each one a sacred rhythm in its endless cycle of life, death, and renewal. The faithful believe that to live in harmony with the seasons is to live in rhythm with the divine pulse of the world itself.
  

## Beliefs:

### The [[Lore/Arctree]] is the Source of All Life.
- Every creature, plant, and soul is a leaf or root of the same divine organism.

### Nature is Divine Will.
- Storms, droughts, growth, and death are not punishments or rewards but expressions of the [[Lore/Arctree]]’s balance.

### Souls Return to the Roots.
- When mortals die, their spirits travel through invisible veins of energy back to the [[Lore/Arctree]], where they rest and are reborn in new forms.

### The Seasons Are Sacred Rhythms.
- Each season marks a divine phase; growth, flourishing, decay, and dormancy. All necessary for balance.

### Magic Is Sap Made Manifest.
- Magic is said to be the sap of the [[Lore/Arctree]] flowing through the world, granting mortals a share of its power.

### The [[Lore/Arctree]] Speaks in Dreams and Signs.
- Visions, rustling leaves, strange animal behavior, or natural patterns are believed to be its whispers.

## Tenets:

### I. Preserve Life, Do Not Dominate It.
- Cultivate, protect, and coexist with nature rather than subjugating it.

### II. Honor the Cycle.
- Birth, death, and decay are sacred. Do not fear endings; they are roots to new beginnings.

### III. Do Not Spill Blood Needlessly.
- The wise hand prunes with care, for every branch is bound to the Root.

### IV. Act as the [[Lore/Arctree]]’s Steward.
- Restore what is broken, heal what is wounded, and teach others to walk gently upon the earth.

### V. Seek Harmony, Not Perfection.
- All things have a place in the weave; even those that seem corrupt or wild.

### VI. Listen to the World.
- Silence and observation are holy acts. The [[Lore/Arctree]]’s voice is subtle, and it speaks through the rustle of leaves and the rhythm of wind.
  

## Religious Practices

### Meditation
- Meditation is of great importance to [[Lore/Arcists]]. Meditation in nature is one of the most effective ways to hear the [[Lore/Arctree]]'s breath.

### Rootbinding
- Creating rings, clothing and other forms of trinkets from nature connects us with the [[Lore/Arctree]].
